---

## 🎯 Goal & Objective
Build a clean, premium, high-converting creative portfolio website that positions Onuabuchi Chidera as a premier **Visual Designer & Video Editor**. Visitors must understand within **5 seconds**:
1. **Who you are** (Onuabuchi Chidera)

3. **Why they should hire you** (Proven commercial track record, 45M+ organic video views, 5+ years experience, and rigorous artistic direction)

## 📝 PHASE 4 — Case Study Formula (The 5-Step Structure)

Every featured project must follow this rigorous narrative structure:
1. **The Context**: Client name, release year, services provided, tools used, and exact role.
2. **01. The Challenge**: What specific business or creative problem needed solving? (e.g. fragmented branding, 60 hours of raw footage, low retention rates).
3. **02. The Approach**: What strategic and aesthetic decisions did you make? (e.g. custom film emulation LUT, syncopated rhythm, Swiss 12-column grid).
4. **03. The Work / Visual Highlights**: High-definition hero video/imagery, responsive gallery, and detail closeups.
5. **04. The Result & Business Impact**: Measurable outcome (e.g. 3.2M impressions, $4.5M seed round closed, 68% retention rate).

## 🎨 PHASE 5 — Visual System & Design Tokens

- **Background**: Deep Obsidian `#111111` / Surface `#161616` / Cards `#1c1c1c`
- **Accent Color**: Electric Volt Lime `#D4FF00`
- **Display Typography**: `Anton` / `Bebas Neue` (Giant Hero Title) & `Syne` (Section Headings)
- **Body Typography**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Inter` (Ultra-clean, modern legibility)
- **Metadata Typography**: `JetBrains Mono` / `Space Mono` (Timecodes, project specs, dates)
- **Visual Restraint Rule**: Let the work provide the motion and color; keep background chrome minimalist and dark.

---

## 📱 PHASE 6 — Responsive Breakpoints & Testing

- **Desktop Large**: 1440px+
- **Desktop Standard**: 1280px / 1024px
- **Tablet**: 768px – 1023px
- **Mobile**: 375px – 767px (Ensure pill portrait scales gracefully and giant typography remains punchy)

---

## 🚀 PHASE 7 — Launch & Deployment Workflow

1. Push all code and assets to GitHub: `https://github.com/MelodyInMyHeart/My-Portfolio-site`.
2. Connect to **Vercel** or **Netlify** with automatic CI/CD deployment on git push.
3. Configure custom apex domain (e.g. `chidera.design` or `onuabuchi.com`).
4. Perform final live testing (video loading speeds, mobile menu toggle, contact form submit, SSL certificate).
